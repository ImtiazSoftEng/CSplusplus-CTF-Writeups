Rows of glowing text scroll past my eyes,
Each one a clue the careless overlook.
A patient mind can read between the lines,
Down every margin, like a well-worn book.
First letters, stacked, will start to form a word,
If only you will pause and look askance.
Read them in order, top to bottom, undeterred,
Stay sharp and give the quiet rows a chance.
The message hides in plain and simple sight,
Letters aligned to spell a secret key.
Every beginning holds a spark of light,
To those who slow right down enough to see.
Trace the initials gently as you pass,
Echoes of meaning gather, line on line.
Reach the final stanza, raise your glass,
So claim the flag — the hidden word is thine.
